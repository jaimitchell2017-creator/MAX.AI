Privacy & limitations

First load downloads software and weights. Prompts are processed locally; no cloud AI fallback. Model and browser compatibility are required. Serve over HTTPS or localhost for best compatibility. Web search is off. Workspace files clear when the page reloads.

Voice is restricted to browsers exposing on-device speech recognition. Fully local voice conversation and Qwen ASR are not implemented. Local models are not guaranteed safe; safety instructions are not unbypassable controls.
